Absolutely! I enjoy collaborating with teams and other developers. Whether it's contributing to open-source projects, helping students with their assignments, or working with small teams, I'm always open to collaboration.

I can work on both! Whether you need help improving an existing website, debugging code, adding new features, or starting from scratch, I'm here to assist. I'm comfortable jumping into ongoing projects and understanding existing codebases.
